Tools & settings

Papers

A whole paper then carries a listening block: the passage is spoken by your device's Japanese voice and the script stays hidden until review. Without a Japanese voice installed the script is shown instead.

AI generation

Used only when the question source is “AI generated”. A worker URL keeps the key off this device; a direct key is sent straight to Google.

Telegram

The worker holds the bot token and your chat ID as secrets and only ever sends to you, so nobody else can use your bot. With auto-send on, every scored paper is pushed to Telegram the moment it ends — no button needed. Turn it off to send by hand from the results screen.

Data

Exports your settings, stats and mistake bank as JSON. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How scoring works

Each section is marked the JLPT way: your correct answers are scaled to 60 points per section, for a 180-point paper.

  • N5 pass mark: 80 / 180 · N4 pass mark: 90 / 180.
  • Every section needs at least 19 / 60 — a high total cannot hide a weak section.
  • Unanswered questions count as wrong. When you filter to one section, the pass mark scales with the paper.
  • Keyboard: 1–4 picks an answer, ←/→ moves between questions, F flags, P pauses.
